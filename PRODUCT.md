# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Polish-speaking friends and family (small private group). Users are active travelers planning real upcoming trips together — they come to the app mid-planning, with destinations already in mind. They're motivated, not browsing; they want to log a trip, build a plan, and see the budget come together.

## Product Purpose

A personal cloud-synced travel planner for a small circle. Users create trips, build day-by-day plans, manage packing lists, track budgets with breakdowns and charts, store a reusable travel knowledge base, and view statistics and an achievement map of destinations visited. Firebase provides real-time sync across devices within the group.

## Brand Personality

Energetic, modern, adventurous — the app should feel like the excitement of planning a trip, not like filling in a spreadsheet. Bold enough to match the subject matter; composed enough to stay useful.

## Anti-references

- Not overly playful or childish — no cartoon-like aesthetics, toy-app color palettes, or cutesy iconography
- Not generic travel-booking sites (Booking.com, Trivago) — too transactional and impersonal
- Not corporate / enterprise-feeling — this is a personal product, not a business tool

## Design Principles

1. **Adventure-forward, compositionally calm** — energy comes from the trip content and brand color, not from visual noise; the UI stays structured so the destinations feel alive
2. **Intimate scale** — built for a small circle, not a crowd; it can afford personality over generic clarity, and delight over pure efficiency
3. **Planning empowers** — complex trip data (multi-day plans, budgets, packing lists) should feel organized and achievable, never daunting; hierarchy and grouping do the heavy lifting
4. **Show the journey, not just the form** — wherever possible, surfaces should feel like they're about real places and experiences, not abstract data entry
5. **Polish context, global destinations** — the UI is Polish-language and built for a local circle, but destinations are international; the aesthetic should feel worldly, not provincial

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal WCAG requirement stated. Minimum target: WCAG AA contrast on all text. Reduced-motion alternative for all animations (the app has fade-in transitions and hover effects throughout). Color should not be the sole differentiator for status or type information.
